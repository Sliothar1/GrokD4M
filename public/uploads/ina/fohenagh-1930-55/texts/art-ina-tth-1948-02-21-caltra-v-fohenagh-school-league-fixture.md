# Caltra v Fohenagh, schools league semi-final

Tuam Herald, 21 Feb 1948, page 3.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19480221.1.3

Transcribed from the page image.

---

The School Leagues semi-final between Caltra and Fohenagh was fixed for Caltra on the 29th February at 3 p.m. Miss S. Ruane, Ballinasloe, was appointed referee. The final was fixed for Galway on the 14th March if Caltra win and for Fohenagh on the 21st March if Fohenagh win, when Oranmore meet the winners.
