# Tim Sweeney (Fohenagh) in the Galway pen-pictures

Tuam Herald, 3 Sep 1955, page 6.

Tim Sweeney (Fohenagh). Age 24; height, 5' 11"; weight, 12st. 4lbs. One of the newcomers to Galway senior ranks, Tim jumped into the limelight against Tipperary and Clare in the full forward line. He has given great service to his junior club, and is an elusive and accurate attacker.
