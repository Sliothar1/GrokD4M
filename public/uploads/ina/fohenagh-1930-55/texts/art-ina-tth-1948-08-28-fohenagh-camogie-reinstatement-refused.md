# Galway Camogie Team (Fohenagh reinstatement refused)

Tuam Herald, Saturday 28 August 1948, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives (permalink: see fohenagh-1930-55.txt, TTH 1948-08-28 p2)

Transcribed from the page image (Fohenagh-related opening; the article continues on Board finances and a Connacht final). BOARD/OTHER, camogie.

---

When an application for reinstatement from the Fohenagh Camogie Club was considered at a meeting of the Galway County Camogie Board on Saturday (Miss M. Duggan, chairman, presiding) the members of the Board and representatives of the clubs present were unanimous in agreeing that everything should be done to create a spirit of good sportsmanship in camogie circles in the province where such did not already exist.

The discussion arose when Mr. P. O'Higgins, National Organiser, proposed that Fohenagh's application would be agreed to as had been requested in a letter from the club to the Board.

Mr. M. Hogan, N.T., Caltra, opposed the application and after a discussion the application was rejected.

No decision was taken on a request from the New Inn Club which suggested that a competition would be started in the county for a Perpetual Challenge Trophy, as those present considered that such a competition would interfere with the championship.

Mr. P. O'Higgins said that the New Inn Club were to be complimented on their initiative because competition was essential for any game and it also helped to build good qualities of sportsmanship in opposition to the "win at any cost" spirit.

The Chairman remarked that the rules of the Association held the team responsible for the actions of their supporters, when one of those present remarked that the ruling was very hard on the girls.

It was agreed that the behaviour of Fohenagh's supporters that caused the application for reinstatement to be rejected and that was not the girls fault.
