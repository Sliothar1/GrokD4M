# St Cuan's panel, with Fohenagh players

Tuam Herald, Saturday 2 May 1998, page 24.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19980502.1.24

Transcribed from the page image. Colleges hurling. The page sends St Cuan's to the final; it does not print the result.

---

St. Cuan's, Castleblakeney, will contest the All-Ireland Colleges Senior B hurling final at Croke Park next Saturday against Portlaoise C.B.S., at 1.30. They have already won the Connacht Cup.

THEY'VE already won the Connacht Cup and the "big one" is only an hour away! The St. Cuan's panel, pictured at school yesterday. Front row: Tim Byrnes, joint-manager; Michael Reynolds (Cappataggle), Shane Connelly (Ahascragh), Colm Kennedy (Fohenagh), Cathal Lohan (Fohenagh), Noel Kelly, capt. (Cappataggle), Barry Dolan (Ahascragh), Patrick Hartigan (Ahascragh) and Eamonn O'Donoghue, Principal. Middle row: John Lynch-Lally (Ahascragh), David Kelly (Cappataggle), Aidan Beatty (Loughrea), Joe Hennelly (Ahascragh), Gary Finnerty (Ahascragh), Declan O'Dea (Padraig Pearse's), Trevor Kennedy (Ahascragh), Shane Ruane (Padraig Pearse's), Mark Ryan (Ahascragh) and Anthony Griffin (Ahascragh). Back row: Niall Bannerton (Ahascragh), Paul Gately (Ahascragh), Brian Lynskey (Fohenagh), John Ryan (Ahascragh), Eoin Kenny (Padraig Pearse's), Owen Lynskey (Fohenagh), Brendan Geraghty (Fohenagh), Brian Laffey (Ahascragh) and Joe Skehill (Abbeyknockmoy). Not in picture are star forward Joe Fitzgerald (Ahascragh) and joint-manager Colette Walsh. Photo: Ray Ryan.
