# Caltra Qualify for County Final

Connacht Tribune, Saturday 2 November 1946, page 8. The masthead on the scan prints 2 November 1946. INA files the page as 26 Oct 1946, page 15.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19461026.1.15

Transcribed from the page image. Camogie.

---

CALTRA defeated Fohenagh by one goal to nil in the semi-final of the County Galway senior camogie championship at Caltra on Sunday last in a hectic struggle that kept the crowd on their toes to the final whistle.

The game, though at times over vigorous, was a thrilling spectacle, and was rated as superior to any of the ties in this year's all-Ireland series. Artistic stick-work was the order, and while the teams were well matched, the winners showed that extra skill which has been characteristic of their many victories in the past.

Though for periods disconcerted by the vigorous tactics of Fohenagh, Caltra countered with finesse and were deserving winners.

In a great back division for Caltra, P. Noone played a grand game in the full back position and had able assistants in M. Rock, D. Collins and J. Kilcommons. A. McDermott and B. Finnerty starred at mid-field while M. A. Geraghty and M. Kilcommons were the pick of the forwards. M. Kennedy kept a sound goal.

M. Barrett was a sterling full-back for Fohenagh and was well supported by A. Glynn and A. Madden, while further afield, R. Clinton, M. Madden, P. Hopkins and R. Naughton were always in the picture.

Exchanges were even in the first quarter with both back divisions saving their lines. In the second quarter Fohenagh in a series of hot attacks on the Caltra posts, initiated by R. Clinton and M. Madden, were repeatedly repulsed with M. Kennedy (goal) and P. Noone doing brilliant work for Caltra. Faulty shooting by their forwards robbed Fohenagh of a possible score at this juncture. A. McDermott rallied Caltra and just before the interval, M. Kilcommons was inches wide with a rasping drive.

Caltra dominated the play for most of the second half, but the Fohenagh defence was sound. With time running out the Fohenagh posts were stormed and in a hot encounter A. McDermott was injured but resumed. A scoreless draw was being anticipated when a spirited attack by Caltra ended in M. A. Geraghty driving home a great goal to leave the result: Caltra, 1-0; Fohenagh, nil.

Mr. L. Gohery, vocational teacher, Mountbellew, was an impartial referee.
