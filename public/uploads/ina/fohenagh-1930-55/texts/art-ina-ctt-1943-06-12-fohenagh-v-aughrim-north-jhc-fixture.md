# Fohenagh v Aughrim Sarsfields at Kilconnell

Connacht Tribune, 12 Jun 1943, page 14.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19430612.1.14

Transcribed from the page image.

---

Fohenagh and Aughrim Sarsfields—old rivals on the hurling field—will meet at Kilconnell on Sunday, 13th, at 4 p.m. in the North junior hurling championship. [..] Keegan, Ballinasloe, will be referee.
