# Skehana Beat Fohenagh in Re-play.

Tuam Herald, Saturday 30 August 1952, page 1.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19520830.1.1

Transcribed from the page image.

---

There were some hectic passages in the re-play of the County Intermediate Hurling championship semi-final between Skehana and Fohenagh at Castleblakeney on Sunday.

In the first game a fortnight earlier, Fohenagh earned a last minute draw with a goal from a penalty.

The first half of the re-play was fairly evenly contested, but on turning over, Skehana pulled out to win in convincing style. Final scores were:

Skehana, 3-7; Fohenagh, 2-1.
