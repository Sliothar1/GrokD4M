# 'Fohenagh is definitely in the North Board'

Connacht Tribune, 12 Jun 1943, page 14.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19430612.1.14

Transcribed from the page image.

---

[Extract; OCR checked against image, partly garbled] Were there any boundaries laid down? Chairman: I don't know what boundaries were laid down, but Fohenagh is definitely in the North Board. Mr. J. Whelan, Secretary: It's much more convenient for Kilgerril to play in the East Board.
