# The referee question

Connacht Tribune, 16 May 1942, page 9.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19420516.1.9

Transcribed from the page image.

---

[Fohenagh-related part of a longer County Board report; read from the page image]

... the County Junior Hurling Semi-Final between Claregalway and Fohenagh.

T. Murphy: It is a shocking thing that teams should be allowed in to a County Board meeting to object to a referee whom the Board selects.

J. Muldoon: In the South every Club is looking for Mr. Cullen to referee.

Referee Question.

T. Murphy: If you appoint another referee now Fohenagh can come along next week and object again and who could blame them?

The Rev. Bro. Declan, Loughrea: Would it be fair to ask the reason for the objection?

W. Morris: You have only to go back to the Sports Ground two years ago to find the reason. The Chairman and Mr. Cullen know the reason.

T. Murphy: Fohenagh did not know anything about this objection to the referee. Mr. Cullen was appointed to referee the game and he should be the man to referee it.

M. Cullen: I will go anywhere and referee any match if the Board ask me despite any objection.

P. Ruane (Claregalway): We did not receive notice that this game was fixed for May 17th and we will not play unless the referee is changed. If the referee is not changed we are giving the match to Fohenagh and we will withdraw from the County Championship.

Chairman: If we leave the referee as he is will the Board take full responsibility for anything that may happen at the match?

T. Murphy: Don't we always take full responsibility?

Chairman: I rule so that the match be played next Sunday and that M. Cullen be referee.
