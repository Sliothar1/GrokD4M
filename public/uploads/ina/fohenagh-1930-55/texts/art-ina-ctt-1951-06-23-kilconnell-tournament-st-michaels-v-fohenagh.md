# St. Michael's v Fohenagh, Kilconnell Tournament

Connacht Tribune, 23 Jun 1951, page 19.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19510623.1.19

Transcribed from the page image.

---

Kilconnell Tournament Final and Sale of Work on June 29th, 1951 (in aid of Church Renovation Funds). HURLING DRAWS: Killimordaly v. Skehanagh at 3 p.m.; St. Michael's v. Fohenagh at 4 p.m.; Final at 5.30 p.m. A Silver Cup and expensive Set of Medals will be presented to the winners. Grand Drawing of Prizes on Field during interval before Final.
