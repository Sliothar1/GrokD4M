# Fohenagh v Ballinasloe Mental Hospital fixed

Tuam Herald, 9 Aug 1947, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19470809.1.2

Transcribed from the page image.

---

The County Championship semi final, Fohenagh v. Ballinasloe Mental Hospital was fixed for Ballinasloe on August 17th at 4 o'clock. Miss C. Mulholland was appointed referee. The County Final will be played in Galway on August 24th when Erin's Hopes meet the winners.
