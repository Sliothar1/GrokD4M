# Sweeny scores 12 points

SWEENY SCORES 12 POINTS AS FOHENAGH ADVANCE TO COUNTY S.H. FINAL

Fohenagh 3-19; Killimordaly 6-4

Kenny Park, Athenry, on Sunday, in a hard-hitting, fast and exciting game, Fohenagh defeated Killimordaly in the Senior Hurling Championship semi-final and so qualified to meet Turloughmore in the decider at Pearse Stadium.

Played in ideal conditions the game brought out the best in both sides, and though the up and coming Killimordaly side was beaten they showed great fighting spirit up to the final whistle and gained much in experience from this game.

Fohenagh, the more experienced and better balanced side, deserved every bit of their six points win and they always pulled out the extra bit that was needed when pressure was hardest.

Half-time score: Fohenagh 2-10; Killimordaly 1-4.

Fohenagh once again had the man of the match in Tim Sweeney who played himself to a standstill at right half-forward. His accurate shooting from play and from frees was a treat to watch. He scored six points in succession during the second half. Frank Madden had one of his worst games to date in the Fohenagh goal, conceding six goals.

Scorers: Fohenagh: T. Sweeney (0-12), with F. Glynn, A. Glynn, J. Murray, Manning and M. Coen also on the list. Killimordaly: P. McGann (2-0), F. Connaire (1-1), T. Moran (1-1), T. Flannery (1-1), J. Dilleen (1-0), P. Mitchell (0-1).

Fohenagh: F. Madden, T. Moylett, P. Killilea, M. Moylett, J. Sweeney, J. Moclair, F. Moylett, J. Murray, M. Coen, T. Sweeney, F. Glynn, Manning, A. Glynn, J. Sweeney, S. Carrick.
