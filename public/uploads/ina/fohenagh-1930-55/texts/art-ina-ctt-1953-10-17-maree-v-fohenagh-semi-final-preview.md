# Maree v. Fohenagh Semi-Final

Connacht Tribune, Saturday 17 October 1953, page 22.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19531017.1.22

Transcribed from the page image.

---

THE Intermediate County semi-final between Maree and Fohenagh will be played at Loughrea on Sunday at 3.15 p.m.

The winners will play Craughwell who have a bye into the final.

In their first and second round matches against Ardrahan and Killimor, Maree have shown grand form and a return to something like the hurling for which this famed parish was renowned.

Their opponents, Fohenagh, have likewise given two splendid displays in ousting the two western teams, Moycullen and Claregalway, and will be all out to make it a treble of successes over West Board opponents.

Both sides have some up and coming performers and in Tim Sweeney the East Boarders have one of the best scoring forwards in any grade in the County. He will have a foe quite worthy of his mettle in James Burke, a hard tackling back, who will not be easily beaten.
