# Fohenagh among Galway's best camogie teams

Tuam Herald, 14 Dec 1946, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19461214.1.2

Transcribed from the page image.

---

CAMOGIE. Miss Delia Heffernan, formerly [of] Timnee, Moylough, is now [secretary] of the County Galway [Camogie] Club. The best teams in the County so far are Ballinasloe, Caltra, Tuam, and Fohenagh.
