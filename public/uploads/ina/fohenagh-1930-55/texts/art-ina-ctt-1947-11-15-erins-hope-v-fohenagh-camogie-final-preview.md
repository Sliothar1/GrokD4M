# Co. Galway Camogie Final at U.C.G.

Connacht Tribune, Saturday 15 November 1947, page 20 (printed page 9).
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19471115.1.20

Transcribed from the page image. Camogie preview. The referee's name at the end is only partly legible.

---

THE Galway County Senior Camogie Final between Erin's Hope (Galway) and Fohenagh will be played at U.C.G. Park on Sunday (3 p.m.) and with many of the county stars in action the game promises to provide first-class fare.

Hopes, former holders of the championship, have had victories over Oranmore and Maree and will have six of the Galway county team—the Misses M. Duggan, C. Mulholland, J. Melvin, C. Griffin, F. Coen and D. Heffernan—to give strength and balance to the side. In Fohenagh, however, they will meet the strongest possible opposition they ever had to face.

This Fohenagh team, now making their first appearance in a county final, have gained confidence by defeating last year's champions, Caltra, in the area semi-final and by the victories over Tuam, Ballymacward and Ballinasloe Mental Hospital. Rita Clinton, centre field star of the county team should give them a big centre pull over Hopes and the team will also have the assistance of M. Madden and M. and A. Glynn of the county team. In addition to the above-named players Hopes will be assisted by M. McQuillan, N. O'Connell, N. Toury, A. Melvin and J. Lynch, while Fohenagh, in addition to those above named will have B. Clinton, M. Barrett, L. Madden, P. Hopkins, J. Glynn, K. Naughton, M. Farrell, A. Madden. The referee will be Mrs. [...] Casley, Chairman of the County Board. [U.C.G.] and Ballinasloe Mental Hospital will play a challenge ([time illegible]) prior to the county final, and a County Board meeting will be held later.
