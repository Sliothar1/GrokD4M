# Fohenagh Win J.H. Title

Connacht Tribune, Saturday 1 October 1949, page 6.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19491001.1.6

Transcribed from the page image.

---

Scores of "fans" rushed into the pitch and chaired "Mickey" Glynn, captain of the Fohenagh Junior Hurling Club when he was presented with the cup on his team's victory (5-8 to 0-3) over Lawrencetown in the East Board Junior hurling final last Sunday in Duggan Park, Ballinasloe.

In presenting the cup, Very Rev E. Hughes, P.P., Lawrencetown, congratulated the winners and thanked them for the clean, sporting game they had played.

Very Rev. Fr. Flannery, P.P., Fohenagh, congratulated both the winners and losers.
