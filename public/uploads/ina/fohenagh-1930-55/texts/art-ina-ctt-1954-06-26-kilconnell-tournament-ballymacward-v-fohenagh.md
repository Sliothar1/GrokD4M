# Ballymacward v Fohenagh, Kilconnell Tournament

Connacht Tribune, 26 Jun 1954, page 12.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19540626.1.12

Transcribed from the page image.

---

[Kilconnell] Tournament, On 29th June, 1954. Draws are as follows: New Inn v. Kilconieron, 2.30; Ballymacward v. Fohenagh, 3.30; Ballinasloe v. Kilconnell, 4.30; Ahascragh v. Cappatagle 'A', 5.30. Valuable Trophies: Set of Silver Medals and a Perpetual Cup. Admission 1/6. Proceeds in aid of Parochial Funds.
