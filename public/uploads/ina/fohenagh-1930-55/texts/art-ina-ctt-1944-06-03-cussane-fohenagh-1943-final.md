# G.A.A. Games In North Galway

Connacht Tribune, Saturday 3 June 1944, page 10 (printed page 5).
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19440603.1.10

Transcribed from the page image.

---

At Menlough on Sunday, St. Patrick's (Ballymacward) defeated Gurteen in the 1944 championship by 4-2 to 1-2, and Cussane defeated Fohenagh in the 1943 final by 5-3 to 2-4. Best for Cussane were the Brodricks, Naughton, Burke, Muldoon and Gill; and for Fohenagh: the Barretts, Naughton and Glynn. Mr. E. Bruen presented the cup on the field.

Athenry and Menlough will meet in the junior football championship at 4 p.m. on Thursday, June 8th, at Monivea. These teams should provide a good game. A meeting of the North Board to make fixtures will be held after the match.
