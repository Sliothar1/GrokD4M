# Tony Kirwan, first Fohenagh All-Ireland medal

Tuam Herald, Saturday 23 October 1993, page 20.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19931023.1.20

Transcribed from the page image. National Hurling League, Galway v Wexford, Kenny Park, Athenry.

---

A dramatic incident from the Galway-Wexford National Hurling League tie at Kenny Park, Athenry, on Sunday. In the 26th minute of the first half, Galway right full-forward Liam Turley of Castlegar made a solo run towards goal but was caught by a Wexford hurley in the tackle. Liam received a cut between his eyes which necessitated five stitches and he had to be withdrawn from the action, with Tony Kirwan (Fohenagh) taking his place. Photos: Ray Ryan.

Galway's seven-point win over Wexford at Kenny Park.

Tony Kirwan, the first Fohenagh man to win an All-Ireland hurling medal in any grade, tore into the game with gusto and though he twice shot wide he was fully deserving of the grand point that eventually came his way in the closing stages, and the big crowd loved him for it.

Galway line, in part: L. Turley, C. Helebert (2-0). T. Kirwan (0-1, on for Turley).
