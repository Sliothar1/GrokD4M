# Fohenagh Win Title

Irish Press, Monday 17 November 1947, page 9 (printed page 8).
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=IPR19471117.1.9

Transcribed from the page image. Camogie. The HITLIST search snippet read the score as "3-1; 0-0". The page itself prints 3-1 to 3-0.

---

Fohenagh, 3-1; Erin's Hopes, 3-0.

BRILLIANT individualism of Rita Clinton and excellent understanding among the forwards won for Fohenagh the final of the 1947 County Galway Camoguidheacht Senior Championship when they defeated Erin's Hopes at Galway, 3-1 to 3-0, yesterday.

Scorers were:—For Fohenagh: R. Clinton (2-1) and F. Hopkins; and for Hopes: C. Mulholland (2-0) and D. Heffernan.
