# Gort Loses Intermediate

Clare Champion, Saturday 19 July 1952, page 10.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CCH19520719.1.10

Transcribed from the page image. This short item sits at the foot of the report on the Galway senior final at Ballinasloe (Castlegar v Ardrahan-Gort), so the curtain-raiser was played at Ballinasloe on Sunday 13 July 1952.

---

GORT LOSES INTERMEDIATE.
Fohenagh, 7-5. Gort, 0-3.

The curtain-raiser to the big match provided very poor fare, as Gort were completely outclassed by a much superior side in Fohenagh.
