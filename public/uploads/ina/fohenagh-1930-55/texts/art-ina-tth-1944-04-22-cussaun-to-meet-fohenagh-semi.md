# Cussaun qualify to meet Fohenagh

Tuam Herald, 22 Apr 1944, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19440422.1.2

Transcribed from the page image.

---

JUNIOR HURLING SEMI-FINAL. Cussaun qualified to meet Fohenagh in the semi-final of the North Board Championship when they defeated Killimoredaly at Tiaquin on Sunday last by one point—Cussaun 10 points, Killimoredaly 9 points. Mr. Thomas Murphy refereed. [from the HITLIST snippet, checked against the page image]
