# Fohenagh v Kiltulla, tournament

Connacht Tribune, 8 Oct 1955, page 12.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19551008.1.12

Transcribed from the page image.

---

The following teams qualify and the draws are as follows: Kilconnell v. Killimordaly, [time]; St. Killian's v. Cormac's, Loughrea, 2.30 p.m. S.T.; Fohenagh v. Kiltulla, 3 p.m. S.T.; Closetoken v. Cappatagle, 3.30 p.m. S.T. Winning team receives Seven Wrist Watches; Runners-up receive Seven Hurleys.
