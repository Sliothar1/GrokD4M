# Camogie county final fixed: Fohenagh v Erin's Hope

Tuam Herald, 8 Nov 1947, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19471108.1.2

Transcribed from the page image.

---

U.C.G. Camogie Club have arranged to play the final of the Co. Camogie senior championship at College Park, Galway, on Sunday, November 16th at 3 o'clock. The finalists are Fohenagh and Erin's Hopes (Galway). U.C.G. will meet Ballinasloe Mental Hospital at 2 o'clock in a challenge game. The final of the Co. Championship should be a close affair while the challenge game should also be interesting. Hopes qualified for the final by defeating Oranmore and Maree, while Fohenagh defeated Caltra and Ballinasloe. A meeting of the Co. Camogie Board will be held after the games.
