# Fohenagh camogie objection withdrawn

Tuam Herald, 16 Nov 1946, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19461116.1.2

Transcribed from the page image.

---

COUNTY BOARD MEETING. At a meeting of the Galway County Board held in Tuam after the match (Miss J. Heffernan, chairman, presiding), an objection lodged by the Fohenagh Club against the awarding of the county semi-final to Caltra was withdrawn. It was decided to publish a list of the names of those who subscribed to the training fund for the county team this year.
