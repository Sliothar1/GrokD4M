# Ahascragh Notes: G.A.A. (Kilconnell hurling tournament)

Westmeath Independent, Saturday 4 July 1953, page 3.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=WMI19530704.1.3

Transcribed from the page image (Fohenagh paragraph only).

---

The concluding games in the annual Kilconnell hurling tournament were played on Monday. In the semi-finals Fohenagh proved too strong for a weak Balylmacward XV, while Cappatagle No. 2 defeated Castleblakeney. In a thrill-a-minute final, Fohenagh slowly but surely gained the upper hand over their traditional opponents. The Sweeney trio ably assisted by the Glynn brothers were the mainstay of the Fohenagh team, while schoolboy Laly's display in goal was first-class. Best for Cappatagle were Byrnes, Hynes and McNamara. If Fohenagh can maintain this form they will go a long way in the county championship. In the North Galway J.F.C. tie, at Ahascragh, on Sunday, the home side ran out easy victors over an under-strength Caltra team, scoring 1-6 without reply.
