# Life suspension for Fohenagh camogie team (column note)

Munster Express, Friday 28 May 1948, page 3.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=MEX19480528.2.3 (verify ID in fohenagh-1930-55.txt)

Transcribed from the page image (column paragraphs only). BOARD/OTHER, camogie.

---

Following unruly scenes, which culminated in an attack on the referee (Mr. K. Phelan, Stradbally) on the occasion of a Gaelic football match at Ballylaneen, Kill, between Kilrossanty and Newtown, recently I have been reading in a Galway paper where a team of the "gentler sex" in that county allowed tempers to become badly frayed at a recent camogie game.

According to the paper I mentioned, the Galway Camogie Board suspended for life a team Fohenagh, as a result of unruly incidents that occurred at a county championship tie between Fohenagh and St. Grellan's, Ballinasloe. The referee reported that followers of the Fohenagh Club attacked an umpire. The club had previously been warned about the conduct of their followers.
