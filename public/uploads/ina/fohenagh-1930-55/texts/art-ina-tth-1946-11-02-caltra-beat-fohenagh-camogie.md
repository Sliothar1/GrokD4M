# Caltra beat Fohenagh (Tuam Herald)

Tuam Herald, 2 Nov 1946, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19461102.1.2

Transcribed from the page image.

---

Caltra Beat Fohenagh. Caltra beat Fohenagh by a goal at Caltra on Sunday last in what was easily the best game of the County Championship. Caltra have now qualified for the County Final, which will be played in Parkmore on Sunday, Nov. 10th.
