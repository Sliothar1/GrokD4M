# Kilconly 2-8, Fohenagh 0-4

Tuam Herald, Saturday 15 October 1960, page 8.

Transcribed from the page image. Junior football semi-final at Mountbellew.

KILCONLY 2-8; FOHENAGH 0-4

Kilconly had little difficulty in defeating Fohenagh in the junior football semi-final at Mountbellew on Sunday last. With a winning margin of ten points, the North Board champions gave a polished display throughout, and there was little chance at any stage in the game that Fohenagh would spring a surprise.

Although Kilconly were masters in most sectors, the game was by no means a one-sided battle. Football was of a good standard, and close marking and tough exchanges provided good fare for the large attendance.

Playing in top gear from the start, Kilconly never relaxed and, although playing against the wind in the first half, they did most of the attacking and led by 1-4 to 0-2 at the interval. Again in the second half, Kilconly played with great spirit.

Half-time: Kilconly 1-4; Fohenagh 0-2.
Final: Kilconly 2-8; Fohenagh 0-4.

Liam Manning, Gerry Kelly and Frank Madden were best in attack, while county hurling stars Tim Sweeney, Jim Moclair and Tom Glynn, with Mick Coen and Vincent Coughlan, were best of a hard-hitting defence. J. Sweeney is on the Fohenagh line.

Fohenagh: M. Moclair; J. Bleaheen, M. Coen, V. Coughlan; T. Glynn, J. Moclair, T. Sweeney; F. Glynn, K. Donnelly, G. Kelly, L. Manning, J. Sweeney; F. Madden, J. Kelly, A. O'Gorman.

Referee: Mr. Patsy Geraghty (Ballinasloe).
