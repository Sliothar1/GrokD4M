# Junior Hurling Final.

Tuam Herald, Saturday 4 April 1942, page 2.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=TTH19420404.1.2

Transcribed from the page image. No score and no team list are printed.

---

Fohenagh emerged winners of the North Board Junior Final after an hour's hard hurling with Cussaun. They were superior most of the hour, but great back play and a splendid goalie kept the Cussaun goal intact. There were some great clashes around the centre and on the wings, and the game was played at a fast pace from start to finish. Though hurling was of the hard pulling variety, there was no very rough play, although tempers were short at times. Fohenagh should have put up a bigger score but dawdling with the ball in front of the goal frittered away great chances. It was here that the Cussaun backs' hard pulling saved their citadel many times. In the last quarter, however, Fohenagh added a couple of goals to win well.
