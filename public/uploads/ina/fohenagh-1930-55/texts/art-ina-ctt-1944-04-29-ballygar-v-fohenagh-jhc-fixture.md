# Ballygar v Fohenagh at Mountbellew

Connacht Tribune, 29 Apr 1944, page 10.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19440429.1.10

Transcribed from the page image.

---

At Mountbellew on Sunday, April 30th, Caltra and Newbridge will meet at 3 p.m. in junior football and Ballygar will meet Fohenagh at 4.45 p.m. in junior hurling. Mr. C. Kitt will referee the first game and Mr. J. [Rafter?] will referee the second. [OCR garbled; read from the image.]
