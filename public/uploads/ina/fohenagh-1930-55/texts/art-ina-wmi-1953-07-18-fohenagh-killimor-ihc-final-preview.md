# Ahascragh Notes: G.A.A. (Fohenagh v Killimor intermediate final preview)

Westmeath Independent, Saturday 18 July 1953, page 3.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=WMI19530718.1.3

Transcribed from the page image. The next paragraph in the column ("HOME") calls Miss Maureen Madden "a prominent member of Fohenagh and Galway Camogie teams" before she emigrated to the U.S.A. about four years earlier. Her family and home details are left out here on purpose (privacy rule).

---

G.A.A.

Fohenagh hurlers who have gone from strength since their semi-final victory over Mullagh are confident that they can defeat Killimor in the intermediate final at Ballinasloe on Sunday at 4-30 p.m. Certainly if Fohenagh can produce the form they have displayed in various tournaments during the summer they should add the championship to the Kilconnell cup. At Ahascragh on Sunday last the local team chalked up yet another victory when they defeated Newbridge in a North Board tie by 1-6 to 0-3. The Ahascragh club, which was revived recently after a lapse of a number of years, promises to be a force to be reckoned with in North Board activities.

HOME (extract)

Miss Maureen Madden ... Prior to her emigration, Miss Madden was a prominent member of Fohenagh and Galway Camogie teams and maintained her interest in the game while in America.
