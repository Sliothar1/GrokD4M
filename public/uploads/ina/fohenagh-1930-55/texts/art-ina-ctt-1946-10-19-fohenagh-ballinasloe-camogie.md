# Fohenagh camogie romp past Ballinasloe

Connacht Tribune, 19 Oct 1946, page 6.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19461019.1.6

Transcribed from the page image.

---

Camogie — Fohenagh camogie team had a runaway victory over Ballinasloe team in Duggan Park, last Sunday. Fohenagh's easy win was not to be wondered at as three of their team played on the County selection in Belfast against Antrim on the previous Sunday. Very Rev. Father M. O'Connor, P.P., Fohenagh, who took a big interest in the team's training was on the side line and was proud of his team's convincing win over the pick of the town.
