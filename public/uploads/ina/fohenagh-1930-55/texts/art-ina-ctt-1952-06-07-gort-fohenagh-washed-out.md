# Gort v Fohenagh washed out

Connacht Tribune, 7 Jun 1952, page 9.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19520607.1.9

Transcribed from the page image.

---

As a result of the hold up the second match between Gort and Fohenagh did not get under way until 5.30, precisely the time when it should have finished. Six minutes later it was washed out by a [cloud]burst and called off before any score was registered.
