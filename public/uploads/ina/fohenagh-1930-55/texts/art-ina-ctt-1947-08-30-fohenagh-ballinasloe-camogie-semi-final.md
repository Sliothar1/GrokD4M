# Fohenagh Qualify for Co. Camogie Final

Connacht Tribune, Saturday 30 August 1947, page 19 (printed page 9).
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19470830.1.19

Transcribed from the page image. Camogie.

---

BY defeating Ballinasloe Mental Hospital by one point (2-3 to 2-2) at Ballinasloe on Sunday Fohenagh qualified for the county camogie final against Erin's Hope at Galway (4) on Sunday. It was a lucky victory, for the reason that Ballinasloe measured up well in every respect except that there was a lack of understanding among the forwards.

From the throw-in Ballinasloe broke away and were soon in the Fohenagh goalmouth where O. Hanniffy (Ballinasloe) was inches wide with a great shot at the goal. Fohenagh's centrefield star, Rita Clinton, doubled on the puck out and sent to A. Glynn who placed the ball in the Ballinasloe goal-mouth which was, however, saved by A. Costello (Ballinasloe) under pressure. Rita Clinton received the clearance and from 40 yards shaved the posts with a great shot. Clashes were hard and excitement ran high as both teams fought for a score. M. Madden (Fohenagh) rendered sterling service in checking every attempt made by Ballinasloe to score. Exchanges at centre-field were over before M. Glynn opened the scoring with a point for Fohenagh to which a goal was added before K. Lyons (Ballinasloe) opened the account for the home team with a point from a 25. Fohenagh enjoyed a slight pull at centre for a few minutes before half-time and K. Naughton scored a goal to which Rita Clinton added a point from 40 yards out. Ballinasloe fought back gallantly and were rewarded when O. Hanniffy scored a goal after receiving from Patty Mullins who, well supported by K. Lyons and B. McDonagh, were holding the Fohenagh centre-field line. Just before the half-time whistle R. Clinton shot wide from a 25. The half-time score was: Fohenagh, 2-2; Ballinasloe, 1-1.

GALLANT LOSERS

On the resumption Ballinasloe broke away and pressed hard for scores which did not come until midway through when from a 40 yards free Ballinasloe scored a point. E. Shaughnessy (Ballinasloe) was replaced by M. Morgan and Patty Mullins was changed from centre to full forward before the full-time whistle. The change paid dividends for Ballinasloe as P. Mullins after receiving from K. Lyons added a goal to the total. Ballinasloe then went all out to obtain the lead but B. Clinton, M. Barrett and M. Madden saved the day for Fohenagh before Rita Clinton had Fohenagh's only second-half score, a point from centre-field and Ballinasloe were gallant and unlucky losers.

Best for Fohenagh were: The Clinton sisters, M. Madden, M. Barrett and P. Hopkins, while Ballinasloe were best served by M. Keogh, P. Mullins, A. Costello, K. Lyons, O. Hanniffy and B. McDonagh.

Ballinasloe: A. Costello, E. Shaughnessy, K. Cunningham, M. Morrissey, M. Keogh, P. Mullins, B. McDonagh, K. Lyons, M. Naughton, P. and O Hanniffy, M. Noone.

Fohenagh: P. Clinton, L. Madden, M. Barrett, A. Madden, M. Madden, P. Hopkins, R. Clinton, J. Glynn, A. Glynn, M. Farrell, M. Glynn, K. Naughton.

Miss C. Mulholland (Galway) refereed.
