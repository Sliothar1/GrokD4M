# Fohenagh v Claregalway, 1941 junior semi-final, at Athenry

Connacht Tribune, 23 May 1942, page 10.
Courtesy of Irish Newspaper Archives: https://irishnewsarchive.com/?a=d&d=CTT19420523.1.10

Transcribed from the page image.

---

At Athenry, on Sunday, Clarenbridge and Craughwell [?] will meet (3 p.m.) in the West Galway Junior Hurling Championship and Fohenagh will meet Claregalway in the 1941 Junior Hurling Semi-Final at 4.30 p.m. Both Fohenagh and Claregalway gave some grand exhibitions last year. The winners next Sunday will meet Eyrecourt in the final.
